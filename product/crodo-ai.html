Crodo AI

Developed by Unknown
StartupGlobalfree
ABOUT

Crodo AI is a voice-first AI assistant built specifically for macOS. It enables users to dictate text directly into any application, ask questions using their voice, receive screen-aware assistance, and capture and summarize meeting notes. Designed to streamline desktop productivity, Crodo integrates with Gmail, Google Calendar, and Slack, allowing users to execute tasks and access their workplace context seamlessly through simple global hotkeys without constant app switching.

CAPABILITIES
  • Voice-First System-Wide Dictation
  • Screen-Aware Context Understanding
  • Meeting Capture & AI Summarization
  • Multi-App Integration (Gmail, Calendar, Slack)
