AgentConnect

Developed by Unknown
Startup Global free
ABOUT

AgentConnect is an open-source platform designed for team-wide AI agent orchestration across Slack, Telegram, Discord, and GitHub. It allows teams to connect any ACP-compatible runtime, such as Claude Code, Codex, or DeepSeek, and assign distinct roles, models, workspaces, and permissions. By resolving the single-operator limitation of terminal-based agents, AgentConnect provides a unified console to monitor and coordinate collaborative multi-agent workflows.

CAPABILITIES
  • Cross-platform collaboration (Slack, Telegram, Discord, GitHub)
  • Support for ACP-compatible runtimes like Claude Code & Codex
  • Granular roles, permissions, and workspace management
  • Unified monitoring console for team-wide multi-agent orchestration
