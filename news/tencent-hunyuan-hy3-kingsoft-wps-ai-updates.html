Tencent Open-Sources Hunyuan Hy3; Kingsoft WPS AI 80M MAU, Qingqiu Agent #2 Globally

April 23, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#hunyuan#wps_ai#qingqiu_agent#ai_agents#spreadsheetbench

On April 23rd, Tencent released and open-sourced its #Hunyuan Hy3 preview large language model. It features 295 billion total parameters, 21 billion active parameters, and supports a maximum context length of 256K. This marks the first model trained post-Hunyuan team reconstruction and is touted as the most intelligent Hunyuan model to date.

Separately, Kingsoft Office launched its new generation WPS Multidimensional Table on April 22nd, announcing WPS AI's domestic monthly active users surpassed 80 million. Its new Multidimensional Table engine achieves an average editing response time of 32 milliseconds under millions of rows of data and thousands of concurrent connections. Furthermore, Kingsoft Office's self-developed AI spreadsheet engine, Qingqiu Agent, ranked second globally on the #SpreadsheetBench testing list, trailing only Gemini in Google Sheets, marking the highest achievement for a Chinese AI product on this benchmark.
