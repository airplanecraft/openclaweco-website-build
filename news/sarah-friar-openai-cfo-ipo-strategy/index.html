OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar Reportedly Instrumental in Microsoft Deal, Suggests 2027 IPO Target

May 2, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#openai#microsoft#cfo#ipo#corporate strategy

OpenAI's Chief Financial Officer, Sarah Friar, is emerging as a pivotal strategic force behind the leading AI research company. According to a report by The Wall Street Journal, sources indicate that Friar played a critical role in keeping the highly scrutinized and deeply strategic partnership between #OpenAI and #Microsoft on track. This collaboration has been fundamental in providing OpenAI with essential computing resources and establishing a solid foundation for its technological commercialization and ecosystem expansion.

Beyond her contributions to strategic partnerships, Friar is also demonstrating a long-term vision for OpenAI's financial trajectory. Insiders suggest that she has privately advised company leadership to target 2027 for OpenAI's Initial Public Offering (#IPO). This recommendation underscores Friar's preference for allowing the company ample time to further develop its technology, expand its market share, and optimize its revenue models, thereby aiming for a higher valuation and more sustainable growth upon going public. This measured strategic approach, rather than a rush to market, is expected to provide stronger momentum for OpenAI's enduring success.
