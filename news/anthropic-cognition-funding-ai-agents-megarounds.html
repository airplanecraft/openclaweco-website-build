Anthropic Secures $65B Series H as AI Startups Dominate Weekly Funding Rounds

May 30, 2026|3 Min Read
#anthropic#cognition#openrouter#devin#mcp

Venture funding has always been a world of haves and have-nots, and today, the top-tier AI giants are capturing unprecedented capital. #Anthropic, the 5-year-old generative AI powerhouse, dominated the headlines this week by securing an astronomical $65 billion in Series H funding, catapulting its post-money valuation to a historic $965 billion. San Francisco-based Anthropic announced that Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Sequoia Capital led the financing, with participation from Capital Group, Coatue, D1 Capital Partners, GIC, Iconiq Capital, and XN.

Close on its heels, #Cognition, the creator of the autonomous AI software engineer Devin, secured over $1 billion in fresh capital, pushing its valuation to $26 billion. The San Francisco-based startup's round was led by Lux Capital, General Catalyst, and 8VC, solidifying the market's conviction in AI agent capabilities.

The funding momentum extended to other sectors leveraging advanced AI infrastructure. Atlanta-based Stord raised $250 million in Series F funding at a $3 billion valuation to expand its AI-driven logistics and fulfillment network. Meanwhile, #OpenRouter, an increasingly vital marketplace and router for AI model APIs, secured $113 million in Series B funding led by CapitalG.

San Francisco's Corgi Insurance also made waves, pulling in $106 million for its AI-native insurance platform led by TCV, setting a $2.6 billion valuation just weeks after its initial Series B. Rounding out the top rounds were notable deep tech deals, including a $100 million Series B for fusion energy pioneer Thea Energy, a $100 million Series E for healthcare data platform Garner Health, and a $90 million round for space tech startup Observable Space.

[AgentUpdate Depth Analysis] The extraordinary valuations of Anthropic and Cognition signal a profound structural shift in the AI venture landscape, moving from raw foundational capability to agentic execution. Anthropic’s near-trillion-dollar valuation represents the bedrock infrastructure, while Cognition’s massive $1B inflow for Devin underscores intense market conviction in autonomous AI Agents. Together with OpenRouter's success in model orchestration, we are witnessing the construction of a three-tier Agent ecosystem: foundational LLMs, routing middleware, and specialized agentic applications. This massive capital concentration will accelerate the transition of AI from simple chat interfaces to complex, multi-agent workflows that fundamentally reshape enterprise productivity and the future of software engineering.
