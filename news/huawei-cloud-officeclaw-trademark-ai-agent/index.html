Huawei Cloud Files "OFFICECLAW" Trademark, Hinting at Potential AI Office Agent Push

May 7, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#ai agent#huawei cloud#trademark#enterprise ai#productivity tools

Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. has recently filed applications to register two new trademarks: "#Huawei Cloud OFFICECLAW" and "OFFICECLAW." According to intellectual property information from Tianyancha, both trademarks are classified under international categories of advertising sales and website services. The current status for these applications is "waiting for substantive examination."

The name "OFFICECLAW," especially when coupled with the "Huawei Cloud" prefix, suggests a strategic move by Huawei into the realm of artificial intelligence (AI) office assistants or automation services tailored for enterprise users. The chosen classifications—website services and advertising sales—further support the interpretation that these potential AI agents could be integrated into cloud-based enterprise applications, marketing automation, or customer relationship management (CRM) systems.

Established in September 1987, Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. is legally represented by Zhao Minglu, with a registered capital of approximately 41.04 billion RMB. Its extensive business scope includes the research, development, production, and sales of big data products, IoT solutions, and communication-related products. The company is wholly owned by Huawei Investment Holdings Co., Ltd. This #trademark application aligns with Huawei's ongoing investments in cloud computing, AI, and enterprise services, signaling a potential expansion into #AI Agent applications.
