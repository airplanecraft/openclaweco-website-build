Jeff Dean Departs Google to Found Startup; Hassabis Steps Down as DeepMind CEO

August 6, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#gemini#deepmind#discovery-loop#agi#ai-agent

Google has been hit by a historic executive shakeup. Legendary computer scientist, Chief Scientist of Google Research and #DeepMind, and #Gemini lead Jeff Dean has officially announced his departure to co-found a new startup. Dean, one of Google’s only two Senior Google Fellows (L11 level), is leaving alongside his long-time L11 partner Sanjay Ghemawat, as well as AI pioneers Quoc Le and Oriol Vinyals. This quartet represents one of the most high-powered founding teams in AI history.

Simultaneously, Google is reorganizing its AI command. Demis Hassabis has stepped down as CEO of Google DeepMind to become Chairman of DeepMind and Chief Scientist of Alphabet, shifting his focus to long-term #AGI strategy. His day-to-day operational responsibilities will be assumed by his deputy, Koray Kavukcuoglu, who has been promoted to Senior Vice President reporting directly to Sundar Pichai. Following these announcements, Alphabet shares slid 4%, wiping out $186 billion in market value.

The new startup, named Discovery Loop, is structured as a public-benefit corporation with a mission to automate machine learning, science, and engineering. The founding lineup is staggering: Sanjay Ghemawat co-created core Google infrastructure like MapReduce and Bigtable; Quoc Le co-authored foundational works on seq2seq and Chain-of-Thought; and Oriol Vinyals led deep learning at DeepMind and co-led Gemini. Together, they bridge two decades of massive-scale computing and modern deep learning.

Google CEO Sundar Pichai reportedly tried to retain the group, but they ultimately chose the freedom of a startup. Google has invested in the new entity and will act as its cloud partner. However, this departure means that the trio behind Gemini’s leadership—Jeff Dean, Oriol Vinyals, and Noam Shazeer—have all left Google, dealing a heavy blow to Google’s flagship model initiatives.

Google continues to grapple with the "Innovator's Dilemma" under fierce pressure from OpenAI and Anthropic. The company has suffered a steady drain of top-tier talent, including Transformer co-inventor Noam Shazeer returning to OpenAI, Nobel laureate John Jumper, and core Gemini contributors joining Anthropic. While streamlining DeepMind’s reporting structure may increase execution speed, losing key researchers who pioneered the base architectures threatens Google's competitive edge.

Unlike standard productivity agents, automating scientific discovery requires unprecedented levels of multi-step planning, rigorous tool use, and autonomous closed-loop experimentation. By combining deep systems engineering with advanced neural architectures, Discovery Loop is uniquely positioned to redefine the infrastructure of AI Agent ecosystems, accelerating the transition from generative assistants to fully autonomous scientific collaborators.
