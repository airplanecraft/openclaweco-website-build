OpenAI Reportedly Exploring Strategic Options for Codex, Including Potential Sale

April 21, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#openai#codex#ai model#divestiture#generative ai

According to Sina Finance, #OpenAI, a leading artificial intelligence research and deployment company, is reportedly engaging with advisory firms to explore strategic options for its AI programming model, #Codex, including a potential sale.

Codex is an advanced language model developed by OpenAI, specifically engineered to translate natural language instructions into executable code. It serves as the foundational technology for widely adopted code generation tools such as GitHub Copilot. This exploration of a potential #divestiture could signal a significant strategic realignment or portfolio adjustment within OpenAI's operations.
