Exploring Real AI Agent Job Market: Five Key Roles Open Now, From Prompt Engineering to System Evaluation

May 6, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#ai agent#prompt engineering#agent evaluation#backend development#product management

To accurately gauge the real hiring landscape for AI Agents, relying on generic job postings is insufficient. The most reliable insights come from scrutinizing live application pages on company-hosted boards. A recent screening of listings on May 6, 2026, identified roles that met four rigorous standards:

  • The application page was live and directly accessible.
  • The job description explicitly centered on AI Agents or agentic systems as core work, not merely marketing.
  • The position was remote or clearly accessible online via a current hiring page.
  • The source was a verified company-hosted board or official application page, avoiding scraped mirrors.

This stringent filtering yielded a more precise list than typical "AI jobs" roundups. The five identified roles span distinct facets of the #AI agent stack: reasoning and guardrails, backend runtime, prompt quality, product ownership, and evaluation infrastructure.

Key AI Agent Roles at a Glance

  • AI Agent Architect, Customer Experience (Airtable)
    • Remote Scope: Remote - US
    • Why it matters for AI Agents: Owns how support agents retrieve, decide, act, and stay within guardrails.
  • Senior Software Engineer, Backend (AI Agent) (Cresta)
    • Remote Scope: United States (Remote)
    • Why it matters for AI Agents: Builds the backend reliability, APIs, and scale layer for production AI agents.
  • Prompt Engineer (Netomi)
    • Remote Scope: Toronto, Canada / Remote
    • Why it matters for AI Agents: Designs prompts, tool descriptions, and benchmarks for enterprise CX agents.
  • Senior Product Manager — Agentic AI Experiences (Wizard)
    • Remote Scope: Remote - USA
    • Why it matters for AI Agents: Owns product behavior for a shopping agent across planning, retrieval, and orchestration.
  • Senior AI Engineer, Agentic Evaluation & V&V (Slingshot Aerospace)
    • Remote Scope: Remote
    • Why it matters for AI Agents: Builds evaluation and validation systems for autonomous, tool-using agent workflows.

1. Airtable — AI Agent Architect, Customer Experience

Role Responsibilities:

Airtable is seeking an architect-level professional to establish the technical foundation for its AI-native customer support stack. The job description is exceptionally clear regarding the scope: this individual will be responsible for how support agents reason, retrieve information, make decisions, and execute actions. Key areas of focus include ensuring retrieval accuracy, optimizing automated resolution rates, implementing guardrails, establishing observability, designing prompt architecture, and facilitating integrations with external systems such as billing platforms, CRMs, internal tools, and Airtable APIs.

Why This is a Core AI Agent Role:

This position is distinct from a generic support-ops role merely enhanced with AI. It directly aligns with the classic agent loop:

  • Retrieving the correct context;
  • Deciding on safe and appropriate actions;
  • Executing tasks via tools or APIs;
  • Observing outcomes and potential failures.
