Baidu Smart Cloud Launches Qianfan Token Plan Enterprise: Unified AI Resource Ops

June 24, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#ai-agents#large-models#baidu-qianfan#enterprise-ai#resource-management

Baidu Smart Cloud recently unveiled its enterprise-grade AI productivity subscription service, the Baidu Qianfan Token Plan Enterprise Edition. This service aims to provide businesses with a unified solution for procuring, managing, and operating AI resources, enabling them to leverage AI capabilities efficiently within a fixed budget and accelerate the large-scale adoption of AI across organizations.

Baidu Qianfan has notably become the first to integrate Zhipu GLM-5.2, making it accessible through the Token Plan Enterprise Edition. This integration allows enterprise users and developers to conveniently invoke this advanced model for various applications, including long-sequence task processing, complex reasoning, and coding, without the need for intricate deployment or fine-tuning.

As large language models (LLMs) continue to evolve, AI is transforming from an innovative tool for a few tech teams into a critical enterprise productivity infrastructure. However, its widespread adoption presents new challenges: fragmented procurement of AI tools, difficulties in unified model resource management, unpredictable usage costs, and insufficient organizational operational capabilities. Enterprises are now seeking solutions for unified AI resource management, improved efficiency, and predictable budgeting.

Addressing these pain points, Baidu Smart Cloud introduced the innovative "seat-based + enterprise shared credit package" model with the Baidu Qianfan Token Plan Enterprise Edition. This model offers businesses a more flexible, efficient, and controllable AI resource management solution. Companies can procure seats based on their organizational scale and business needs, leveraging a unified credit pool for resource sharing and flexible scheduling. This transforms a decentralized procurement approach into an enterprise-level AI productivity operation model, shifting from "buying tools" to "operating productivity."

In terms of resource management, the Token Plan Enterprise Edition combines a seat-based system with a Credits points system. This ensures enterprises have immediate access to industry-leading models and benefit from high token supply rates. Furthermore, it provides enterprise-grade operational features such as member management, seat allocation and recycling, shared credit package management, usage statistics, alert notifications, and log viewing. These tools help establish a unified AI resource management system. Compared to traditional pay-as-you-go models, this offers real-time visibility into an organization's overall AI usage, making budgets more transparent.

Regarding model capabilities, the Token Plan Enterprise Edition supports flexible invocation of full-modality and multi-models, consolidating leading capabilities from series like DeepSeek-V4, GLM-5, and Kimi-K2.6. This model matrix will be continuously updated as model capabilities advance. Enterprises can select models freely based on business scenarios, with unified deduction via Credits, eliminating the need to procure and manage multiple model services separately.

Additionally, the Token Plan Enterprise Edition is compatible with various mainstream coding tools and popular AI Agent tools. It broadly covers scenarios such as code development, intelligent office, knowledge management, content generation, and Agent building, helping businesses seamlessly integrate AI capabilities into their existing development and office workflows.

To cater to different enterprise sizes and usage intensities, the Token Plan Enterprise Edition offers multiple tier options, including Light Enjoy, Standard, and Advanced, allowing businesses to flexibly configure seats based on job roles, usage frequency, and business intensity. Baidu Qianfan pledges not to use user data for model training or service optimization, provides enterprise-grade data security management and access control, and ensures service stability through enterprise-level SLA.

Currently, enterprise AI resource management models are evolving from traditional software licensing and SaaS subscriptions to AI capability subscription services. Baidu Qianfan will continue to refine its enterprise-grade AI service system, helping more businesses unlock the value of AI productivity within controlled budgets, visible management, and measurable value, accelerating their journey into the AI-native era.

[AgentUpdate Depth Analysis] Baidu Smart Cloud's Qianfan Token Plan Enterprise Edition, particularly its emphasis on supporting AI Agent tools, holds significant strategic importance in the current LLM application ecosystem. Compared to traditional API calls or SaaS services, this solution not only provides a unified entry point for model capabilities but, more crucially, addresses core pain points in enterprise Agent development and deployment, such as resource fragmentation and unpredictable costs, through its "seat-based + shared credit package" model. While leading Agent frameworks like LangChain and LlamaIndex offer robust building capabilities, underlying model invocation, resource quotas, and cost management remain challenges for enterprise-scale adoption. The Qianfan Token Plan could become a vital "highway" connecting Agent development frameworks with enterprise-grade model resources. It will enable businesses to flexibly allocate computing resources for diverse Agent tasks, for instance, providing sufficient token supply for complex reasoning AI customer service Agents or code generation Agents, while ensuring transparent and controllable overall costs. This will lower the barrier for enterprises to adopt and scale AI Agents, fostering rapid incubation and deployment of more industry-specific Agent applications, and propelling the entire AI Agent ecosystem from proof-of-concept to large-scale production. It is foreseeable that similar unified resource management platforms will become an indispensable component of the AI Agent infrastructure, empowering enterprises to achieve true intelligent operations in the AI-native era.
