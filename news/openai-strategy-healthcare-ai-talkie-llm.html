OpenAI's Strategic Realignment, AI's Impact on Healthcare, and the Ancient Text LLM 'Talkie'

May 1, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#openai#llm#ai strategy#healthcare ai#microsoft

This week's Hard Fork podcast discussed #OpenAI's significant recent strategic shifts, including a loosened partnership with #Microsoft and an aggressive new approach to securing computing power. The episode delved into what these updates signal about OpenAI’s broader business strategy and whether the company can effectively scale while navigating a trial against Elon Musk and addressing investor concerns over missed financial targets.

Additionally, Dr. Adam Rodman, an A.I. researcher and physician from Harvard Medical School and Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, returned to share insights on the profound ways artificial intelligence is transforming how doctors treat patients. Dr. Rodman is an assistant professor at Harvard Medical School.

Finally, the podcast explored an intriguing project: "Talkie," an #LLM specifically trained solely on very old texts. The discussion focused on whether such a model could predict future events, featuring one of its creators, David Duvenaud. Duvenaud is an associate professor at the University of Toronto and a former team lead at Anthropic.

Zhipu AI Reveals "Scaling Pain": Addressing GLM-5 Coding Agent Anomalies Under High Load with Robust System Engineering

Zhipu AI has disclosed its 'Scaling Pain' experience with GLM-5 series models, specifically addressing anomalies like garbled output and repetition in high-concurrency Coding Agent tasks. After weeks of investigation, the team traced the issues to KV Cache race conditions in the PD separation architecture and HiCache loading timing deficiencies. Zhipu implemented strict synchronization protocols and introduced LayerSplit, a hierarchical KV Cache storage solution, which not only resolved the bugs but also significantly boosted system throughput. This highlights the critical role of robust system engineering in scaling AI capabilities.
