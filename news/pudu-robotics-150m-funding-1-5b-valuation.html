Shenzhen-based Pudu Robotics Raises $150M, Valuation Tops $1.5B, Bolstering Commercial Service Robot Leadership

April 24, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#pudu robotics#commercial robotics#service robots#robot funding#ai automation

Pudu Robotics, a Shenzhen-based leader in the commercial service robot sector, has successfully closed a new funding round, securing approximately $150 million. This latest investment significantly boosts the company's total funding to over $300 million, while pushing its valuation beyond the $1.5 billion mark.

Specializing in the research, development, design, production, and sales of commercial #service robots, #Pudu Robotics offers a diverse product portfolio including delivery robots, cleaning robots, and reception robots. The substantial capital infusion underscores robust investor confidence in Pudu Robotics' market leadership and the promising growth trajectory of the commercial service robotics industry.

The newly acquired funds are earmarked to accelerate Pudu Robotics' technological research and development, particularly focusing on core areas such as robot motion control, AI perception, and navigation. Furthermore, the investment will support the company's global expansion efforts, strengthening its competitive edge in the international commercial service robot market. As global labor costs rise and the demand for automation increases, Pudu Robotics' solutions are poised to play an increasingly critical role across sectors like hospitality, retail, and healthcare, driving the intelligent transformation of the service industry.
