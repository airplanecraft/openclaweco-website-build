NVIDIA Profits Leap; Gemini Reaches 900M MAU; SmartBI Debuts Agent BI

May 21, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#nvidia#gemini#smartbi#doubao#agent bi

[AI & Frontier Tech Spotlight]

NVIDIA Releases Stellar Q1 Earnings: Net Profit Skyrockets 211% to $58.3 Billion
NVIDIA released its financial results for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 (Editor's Note: as stated in the original text). The company reported revenue of $81.62 billion, up 85% year-over-year, and a net profit of $58.32 billion, representing a massive 211% YoY increase, solidifying its dominance in the AI hardware space.

Google Gemini MAU Hits 900 Million, Token Processing Volume Grows 7x
At the I/O 2026 developer conference, Google CEO Sundar Pichai announced that Gemini's monthly active users (MAUs) have doubled over the past year to reach 900 million, with daily requests surging over 7-fold. Google now processes over 3.2 exatokens of AI data monthly, a 7x jump from 4,800 trillion tokens a year ago. This growth has fueled a 25% rise in Alphabet's stock this year.

SmartBI Unveils Industry's First Enterprise-Grade #Agent BI "Baize"
On May 20, #SmartBI officially launched SmartBI Baize V5. Positioned as the industry's first Agent BI product, Baize features a dual-engine architecture combining an indicator system with Multi-Agent technologies. Boasting a 99.9% accuracy rate in core analytical scenarios, Baize supports full-link intelligent data analysis and is already deployed at scale in financial, manufacturing, and state-owned enterprises.

User's Attempt to Book Restaurant via Doubao AI Fails Due to Action Loop Gaps
A recent dispute emerged in Zhenjiang when a customer attempted to book a table using Bytedance's Doubao AI app. The customer arrived at the restaurant claiming a "successful reservation," only to be turned away. Doubao's customer service clarified that the app does not support actual restaurant reservations and cannot transmit private user data to third parties. This highlights a growing friction point where users mistake simulated AI conversations for real-world transaction confirmations.

[Corporate & Macro Tech Updates]

Samsung Electronics Union Postpones Strike Following Tentative Pay Agreement
Samsung Electronics and its labor union reached a tentative agreement, temporarily halting a planned strike. The deal includes a 6.2% wage increase and 6 million KRW worth of stock for each employee in the DX (Device eXperience) division. Union members will vote on the proposal from May 23 to 28.

China to Commercially Acquire 200 Boeing Aircraft
According to the Ministry of Commerce, Chinese airlines will acquire 200 Boeing aircraft on commercial terms based on domestic aviation demands. Concurrently, the US will guarantee adequate supplies of engines and replacement parts.

Aier Eye Hospital Pays 524 Million RMB in Back Taxes and Penalties
Aier Eye Hospital announced that following a tax self-inspection, it has completed paying 348 million RMB in back taxes and 176 million RMB in late fees. The company will adjust its fiscal 2025 corporate income tax filing by an additional 232 million RMB.

Luoxin Pharma Fined 1.86 Million RMB for Substandard Azithromycin; Executives Fired
Luoxin Pharma addressed an administrative penalty regarding its substandard Azithromycin for injection, attributing the quality failure to extreme heat during logistics. The company has halted production and scrapped existing inventory. The head of quality has been terminated, and the GM of Shandong Luoxin received a two-rank demotion and pay cut.

[Other Global Highlights]

Russia and China Extend Mutual Visa-Free Transit Policies Until 2027
China and Russia will extend their visa-free policies to December 31, 2027. Russian ordinary passport holders can enter China without a visa for business, tourism, or transit for up to 30 days. Russian Presidential Press Secretary Dmitry Peskov confirmed that Russia will similarly extend visa-free rules for Chinese citizens.

US Markets Rise: ARM Jumps 15%, Apple Hits Record Closing High
On May 20, Wall Street indices closed higher, with the Dow up 1.31%, Nasdaq up 1.54%, and S&P 500 up 1.08%. Driven by AI demand, ARM surged over 15%, Apple rose over 1% to a record high, Tesla jumped 3%, and #NVIDIA rose over 1%.

Sushiro Addresses Hygiene Penalty Over Dirty Tableware
A Sushiro outlet in Hangzhou was fined 60,000 RMB for unlicensed pastry sales and poor tableware hygiene. Inspections revealed substantial water spots and food residue on sashimi plates (10 out of 10 sampled were dirty) and dining utensils. Sushiro responded that corrective measures have been fully implemented.

Huayi Brothers Listed as Dishonest Judgment Debtor
Huayi Brothers, once the premier stock of China’s entertainment industry, was listed as a dishonest judgment debtor after failing to settle a 600,000 RMB court judgment.

[AgentUpdate Depth Analysis] This edition highlights the critical transition of AI Agents from novelty to practical workflows across enterprise and consumer domains. SmartBI’s "Baize" Agent BI, utilizing a Multi-Agent architecture to achieve 99.9% accuracy, demonstrates how vertical agents can overcome LLM "hallucination" in high-stakes corporate environments. Conversely, the "Doubao booking incident" exposes the current gap in consumer-facing AI: despite human-like conversation, current LLMs lack the secure API integration and physical-world action capabilities required for closed-loop services. For the AI Agent ecosystem to mature, scaling compute (NVIDIA) and token processing (Gemini) is only half the battle. The long-term breakthrough lies in standardizing tool-use protocols (such as Model Context Protocol) and secure authentication, transforming agents from passive chat interfaces into autonomous, action-oriented assistants.
