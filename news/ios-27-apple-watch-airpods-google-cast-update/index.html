Bloomberg: iOS 27 to Revamp AirPods and Support Google Cast as Watch Pivots

May 25, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#apple#ios 27#airpods#apple watch#siri

According to the latest report from Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, Apple is gearing up for a significant shake-up of its wearable and software ecosystem. Now over a decade old, the #Apple Watch is facing intensifying competition from nimble rivals like Whoop, Oura, and Google Fitbit. As the health and fitness wearables market pivots toward screenless form factors and specialized tracking, Apple is under pressure to revitalize its smartwatch strategy.

The upcoming #iOS 27 is set to introduce several major changes. Sources reveal that Apple will debut a completely revamped #AirPods control panel, streamlining the currently frustrating management experience. In a surprising move toward openness, iOS 27 will also add default support for Google Cast, a direct rival to Apple's proprietary AirPlay protocol, allowing seamless cross-platform media streaming.

On the health and OS level, watchOS 27 will bring improved heart rate tracking algorithms. However, Apple’s highly anticipated "Mulberry" AI-powered health coach has reportedly run into delays. Additionally, deeper integrations of Genmoji and upgraded Siri features are expected to be unveiled at WWDC 2026, marking a significant milestone in Apple’s ongoing software evolution.

[AgentUpdate Depth Analysis] Apple's moves in iOS 27—especially streamlining AirPods controls and embracing Google Cast—signal a strategic alignment for the upcoming ambient AI Agent era. AirPods are no longer just audio accessories; they are the primary, zero-friction interface for voice-first AI Agents. Simplifying their controls prepares users for continuous, hands-free interaction with on-device LLMs. Furthermore, supporting Google Cast represents a pragmatic concession: in a decentralized, Agent-driven multi-device ecosystem, closed gardens hinder user utility. AI Agents must orchestrate across diverse hardware, and protocol interoperability is key. While the delay of the "Mulberry" AI health coach highlights the massive challenge of building reliable, domain-specific agentic reasoning in clinical health, Apple’s hardware triad—Watch for rich biometric sensing, AirPods for ambient voice interaction, and Apple Intelligence for reasoning—is quietly establishing a powerful edge-AI Agent ecosystem.
