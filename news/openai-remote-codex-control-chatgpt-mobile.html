OpenAI Introduces Remote Codex Control in the ChatGPT Mobile App

May 15, 2026|3 Min Read
#codex#openai#chatgpt#ai-agent#remote-control

OpenAI is significantly enhancing the mobile coding experience by introducing Remote #Codex Control directly within the #ChatGPT app. This strategic integration allows developers to maintain productivity and oversight of their projects even when they are away from their primary workstations.

The new feature enables users to seamlessly monitor and control active coding sessions powered by Codex that are running on a connected desktop computer, all via their smartphone interface. By establishing a robust connection between mobile devices and paired desktop environments, #OpenAI is effectively unifying the developer experience across different hardware platforms.

This advancement represents a major leap in the flexibility of modern AI-driven workflows. It ensures that critical coding tasks can continue smoothly without requiring the developer to be physically present at their desk. The integration of remote access highlights OpenAI's commitment to making advanced AI tools more accessible and adaptable to the dynamic, on-the-go needs of today’s software engineers.
