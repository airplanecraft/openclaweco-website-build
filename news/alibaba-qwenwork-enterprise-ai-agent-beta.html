Alibaba Launches QwenWork Open Beta, Pioneering Enterprise-Grade AI Agents

August 3, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#qwen#qwenwork#ai-agent#enterprise-ai#dingtalk

On August 3, Alibaba officially launched the open beta of its enterprise-grade Agent product, QwenWork (千问办公). Available via web and a standalone PC client, the platform will soon integrate into DingTalk. Users can experience Alibaba's latest flagship model, Qwen 3.8, firsthand during this public testing phase.

Re-engineered from the integration of three distinct products—QoderWork, MuleRun, and Goku—#QwenWork stands as the industry's first solution to simultaneously support desktop Agents, cloud Agents, and enterprise collaborative Agents. This multi-terminal approach aims to redefine both personal and corporate productivity.

Unlike conventional consumer-oriented AI assistants, QwenWork is laser-focused on the enterprise market. Having established initial integration with #DingTalk IM, it plans to connect deeply with real corporate databases and business workflows. For instance, a law firm can transform a senior attorney's process of drafting due diligence reports into an "organizational skill" that junior staff can instantly invoke.

QwenWork also excels in global collaboration. A local team can deploy an Agent to summarize project details, generate English reports, and hand over tasks to overseas colleagues. While the local team is offline, this 7x24 active Agent continues to answer queries in the project channel. Alibaba plans to release mobile apps and an international version soon.

[AgentUpdate Depth Analysis] The launch of Alibaba's QwenWork marks a critical shift for AI Agents from personal productivity tools to the backbone of enterprise operations. By introducing "organizational skills," QwenWork provides a mechanism to crystallize implicit employee knowledge into reusable digital assets. Compared to Microsoft Copilot or Slack AI, Alibaba’s unique competitive edge lies in the deep integration with its massive DingTalk ecosystem and localized workplace dynamics. The "desktop + cloud + collaboration" trio effectively bridges corporate data silos. In the evolving Agent ecosystem, the battleground is shifting from raw LLM parameter sizes to the depth of integration within complex, high-frequency enterprise workflows. QwenWork's open beta represents a pioneering blueprint for the commercialization of enterprise AI Agents.
