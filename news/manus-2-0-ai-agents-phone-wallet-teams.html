Manus 2.0 Makes a Grand Return: AI Agents Equipped with 'Phone Numbers' and 'Wallets' for Enhanced Collaboration

September 30, 2026|3 Min Read
#ai agent#multi-agent#manus#ai productivity#agent framework

Shortly after announcing its return to independent operations, Manus, a pioneer in the AI Agent space, unveiled its first major post-reindependence update: Manus 2.0. This release introduces a new Agent foundation, a creative workbench, and a groundbreaking personal Agent application, signaling a strong re-entry into the AI Agent race.

The timing of the Manus 2.0 launch is particularly noteworthy. Coming just days after Meta's personal Agent, Muse, topped the U.S. App Store free charts, the newly independent Manus returns with its comprehensive product suite. This positions the former acquirer and acquired company as direct competitors in the evolving AI Agent landscape.

Equipping Agents with 'Digital Identities': The Cue Personal Agent App

The new personal Agent app, Cue, is a standalone application designed for both mobile and desktop, sharing infrastructure with Manus. Cue's core innovation lies in endowing each Agent with a unique 'digital identity,' comprising an independent email address, phone number, wallet, and personal computer. These resources enable Agents to send messages externally, make payments within a set budget, execute tasks on their virtual machines, and even answer calls for users, providing call summaries.

This design grants Agents the fundamental capabilities of a human secretary operating online. They can contact external parties, advance processes, and complete transactions. Furthermore, Cue allows users to assign different responsibilities to Agents and group them for collaborative work. For instance, when planning an event, one Agent might find venues, another compile guest lists, and a third draft presentations, with the user making final decisions. This #multi-agent collaboration significantly boosts efficiency for complex tasks. Currently, Cue is in early access and requires an invitation code.

Enhanced Human-AI Co-creation: #Manus Studio Desktop App

The desktop application has been upgraded to Manus Studio, expanding its office and creative capabilities. Beyond standard document, spreadsheet, PDF, and presentation handling, key highlights of this update are its video editing and game development functionalities.

For video editing, Manus Studio provides an intuitive timeline editor, allowing users to directly replace, adjust, and modify segments, images, text, animations, and audio after AI generates an initial video. This 'human-in-the-loop' collaboration addresses the pain point of needing fine-tuned adjustments after AI content generation. A special Alchemy mode simultaneously leverages video generation and code generation capabilities for creative input, animation, and editing. It's noted that Cue's promotional video was created using Manus Studio, by generating it as code and then converting it to video.

In game development, the Game Dev feature offers playable templates, integrating image, video, and programming models. Users can modify code, adjust characters, and alter scenes in real-time while the game runs, then publish it as a web-based game. For multiplayer needs, Manus has integrated cloud computer purchasing and multiplayer configuration directly into the workflow, further extending AI's application in content creation.

Technological Innovation and Market Competition

These functionalities are powered by Manus's new self-developed Agent framework, Cascade. Cascade dynamically integrates specialized capabilities, with official tests showing a 23.2% reduction in Token consumption, a 28.2% decrease in task completion time, and a 32% lower running cost compared to previous systems, significantly boosting efficiency and cost-effectiveness. Automation has also expanded from timed execution to event-triggered actions, responding to conditions like emails, calendar events, or Notion updates.

With the launch of Manus 2.0 and Cue, the personal Agent sector is becoming increasingly crowded. Besides Meta Muse, Qi Junyuan, former head of Doubao PC, has entered the market with Today AI, and OpenAI is rumored to be preparing a persistent Agent. This indicates a widespread industry belief that personal Agents capable of long-term user understanding and service will be a crucial future direction. While personal and enterprise Agents differ in their focus (e.g., personal preferences vs. access management), they share many overlapping tasks, hinting at a potential convergence.

[AgentUpdate Depth Analysis]

The release of Manus 2.0, particularly the innovation in the Cue app of assigning 'digital identities' (phone numbers, emails, wallets) to AI Agents, marks a significant milestone in the evolution of the AI Agent ecosystem. This moves Agents beyond simple task execution tools towards entities with independent 'presence' and 'social capabilities.' Unlike frameworks such as LangChain or CrewAI that focus on tool use, task planning, and multi-agent orchestration, Manus adds the crucial 'media' for agents to interact with the external digital world. This transforms agents from mere extensions of users into independent digital citizens capable of 'living' and 'working' autonomously online.

In the long run, this empowerment of 'digital identity' will profoundly impact the AI Agent landscape. It vastly expands the application scope of agents, from basic information retrieval to complex cross-platform and cross-domain transaction processing, such as autonomous bookings, online shopping, and social interactions. Furthermore, multi-agent collaboration will become more seamless, allowing them to exchange information and hand off tasks via digital communication, forming highly efficient, intelligent, and distributed workflows. However, this also introduces new challenges related to agent identity verification, privacy protection, financial security, and accountability, which will require collaborative solutions from both technology and regulatory bodies. Manus's move undoubtedly paints a more imaginative picture for the future of the AI Agent ecosystem: a highly autonomous and collaborative intelligent society composed of agents with independent digital identities.
