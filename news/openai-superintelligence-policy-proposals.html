OpenAI Unveils Policy Proposals for Superintelligence Era: Higher Taxes, Public AI Fund, Stronger Safety Nets

April 6, 2026|3 Min Read
#superintelligence#ai policy#openai#governance#economic impact

OpenAI, a leading artificial intelligence research organization, has unveiled a series of comprehensive policy proposals aimed at navigating the profound societal and economic shifts anticipated with the advent of #superintelligence. These recommendations address the critical need for proactive #governance and resource management in a future heavily influenced by advanced AI.

Key among the proposals is the call for higher capital gains taxes. This measure is intended to fund a significant portion of the societal transitions required when superintelligent systems begin to automate a wide range of tasks, potentially leading to unprecedented wealth creation but also significant job displacement. By capturing a share of the economic benefits generated by advanced AI, governments could reinvest in public services and support affected populations.

Furthermore, #OpenAI advocates for the establishment of a public AI investment fund. Such a fund would serve multiple purposes: accelerating beneficial AI research and deployment, ensuring equitable access to AI's benefits, and potentially providing a sovereign stake in critical AI infrastructure. This initiative could help democratize the ownership and direction of AI development, preventing undue concentration of power and wealth.

The proposals also emphasize the strengthening of social safety nets. As AI's capabilities grow, the nature of work and economic stability for many individuals may undergo radical transformations. Enhanced safety nets, potentially including universal basic income or robust retraining programs, would be crucial for providing a stable foundation for citizens during these periods of rapid change and adaptation. These measures are designed to mitigate risks of inequality and social disruption, fostering a more resilient society capable of adapting to a superintelligent future.
