Tsinghua PhD Raises Millions for Nexus: The AI-Native Team Collaboration OS

August 3, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#nexus#multi-agent#collaboration#reinforcement-learning#self-evolution

Recently, AI-native startup Singularity Escape secured tens of millions of RMB in seed funding, jointly led by Sparkland Capital and Tsinghua Holdings Capital, with participation from MiraclePlus. The company is developing Nexus, an AI-native team #collaboration operating system designed to enable humans, Agents, tasks, and knowledge to collaborate dynamically on a shared organizational state and self-evolve from real-world workflows.

Founder and CEO Xue Chuanyi, a Tsinghua University PhD, specializes in reinforcement learning and multi-agent systems, with publications in NeurIPS. Xue observed a critical 'collaboration gap' in enterprise AI today. While standalone Agents perform well, their context is locked inside isolated chat windows. This forces humans to act as manual routers, copying and pasting data to align different Agents.

To bridge this gap, Nexus structures organizational tasks, Agents, and tools as interconnected graph objects instead of flat chat histories. A task is no longer just a simple prompt, but a structured object containing goals, dependencies, permissions, and validation criteria. This setup allows incoming Agents to instantly grasp upstream context, transforming AI from a personal chatbot into an integrated organizational resource.

Collaboration and #self-evolution are two sides of the same coin in Nexus. Real-world work feedback drives a closed-loop self-evolution mechanism. Corrections and validation results from humans serve as causal evidence to generate system optimizations (e.g., modifying model routing or prompt structures). These updates undergo rigorous independent evaluation and governance before deployment, ensuring safe, reversible, and measurable system evolution.

[AgentUpdate Depth Analysis] Singularity Escape's Nexus OS directly addresses the enterprise bottleneck of 'isolated agent syndrome.' Unlike code-level #multi-agent frameworks like CrewAI or AutoGen that rely on static workflows, Nexus innovates by shifting from chat-centric UX to an 'organization-state-centric' graph architecture. This transforms AI collaboration from a sequence of APIs into a persistent, stateful operating environment. More importantly, its 'feedback-evaluation-governance' loop presents a highly pragmatic path for on-the-fly Agent evolution without risking alignment failures. By treating AI agents as dynamic team members with structured roles and permissions, Nexus sets a foundational paradigm for the future AI Agent ecosystem, turning individual intelligences into a cohesive, self-improving digital workforce.
