OpenAI Reportedly Allows Employees to Sell Up to $30 Million in Shares in New Tender Offer

May 11, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#openai#stock#liquidity#valuation#employee ownership

Leading artificial intelligence company #OpenAI has reportedly initiated a new opportunity for its employees to sell company shares. This latest tender offer allows eligible employees to divest holdings valued at up to $30 million per individual. The move is part of OpenAI's periodic #liquidity programs, designed to provide early contributors and long-standing team members with a mechanism to realize the value of their equity.

The share sale is anticipated to be conducted through a secondary market transaction, typically involving existing investors or new external parties. Such liquidity events are a common practice among high-growth, privately held technology companies. They enable firms to offer employees a pathway to monetize their stock options and restricted stock units without undergoing an initial public offering (IPO), thereby aiding in both talent attraction and retention.

OpenAI currently boasts a robust private market #valuation exceeding $80 billion, positioning it as a frontrunner in the global AI landscape. Despite the significant sum employees are permitted to sell, such internal liquidity events typically do not negatively impact the company's overall valuation. Instead, they often reinforce employee confidence in the company's long-term trajectory. By capping individual sales, OpenAI balances the need for employee liquidity with the objective of maintaining a stable ownership structure. The company has previously utilized similar mechanisms to provide liquidity to its workforce.
