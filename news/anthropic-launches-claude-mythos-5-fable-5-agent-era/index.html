Anthropic Launches Claude Mythos 5 and Fable 5 for Advanced AI Agents

June 10, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#claude#anthropic#ai-agents#swe-bench#llm

Anthropic has officially unveiled its most powerful flagship models to date: Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5. Fable 5 is the publicly available, safety-aligned version that falls back to Claude Opus 4.8 when safety classifiers are triggered. Mythos 5 is the fully-unlocked, raw version reserved for trusted users, excelling in cybersecurity and biotech research. Crucially, #Anthropic has slashed API pricing by more than 50%, down to $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens.

In software engineering, #Claude Fable 5 scored a groundbreaking 80.3% on SWE-bench Pro, significantly outperforming GPT-5.5 at 58.6%. During a real-world test with Stripe, Fable 5 completed a full migration of a 50-million-line Ruby codebase in just one day—a task that would normally take an engineering team over two months. On the frontend benchmark ViBench, it achieved near-saturated one-shot application generation.

In terms of native vision, Fable 5 and Mythos 5 achieved 29.8% on the GDPpdf document reasoning benchmark. Fable 5 also demonstrated remarkable spatial reasoning by beating Pokemon FireRed purely through raw screenshots without any external navigation scaffolding. Moreover, when integrated with persistent file memory, its success rate in the game Slay the Spire tripled, demonstrating unparalleled long-context focus.

Long context and memory are the highlights of this release. Fable 5 excels in maintaining focus across million-token tasks while leveraging its own notes to self-correct. Anthropic specifically emphasized token efficiency to address the high costs associated with agent-driven deployment in enterprise workflows. Fable 5 also became the first model to surpass the 90% threshold on the Hebbia finance benchmark.

[AgentUpdate Depth Analysis] The launch of Claude Mythos 5 and Fable 5 represents a watershed moment for the AI Agent ecosystem. By driving the SWE-bench Pro score to an unprecedented 80.3%, Anthropic has successfully tackled the critical bottleneck of persistent memory and cognitive drifting in long-horizon tasks. Unlike general-purpose scaling shown in competitors like GPT-5.5, Anthropic’s optimization of Token efficiency and native file-level memory directly addresses the cost and architectural barriers of enterprise deployment. This structural shift moves us past single-turn chat interfaces to autonomous, reliable agentic workflows. It forces the industry to re-evaluate model utility based on execution endurance and memory persistence rather than mere raw context size.
