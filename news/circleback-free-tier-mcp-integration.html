Meeting Note-Taker Circleback Launches Free Tier and MCP Access

August 31, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#mcp#circleback#meeting-assistant#yc

The meeting note-taker market is becoming increasingly saturated. In recent weeks, dictation app Wispr launched its own note-taker and scheduling assistant, and Calendly integrated similar capabilities. Dedicated players like Granola, Read AI, and Fireflies have raised massive venture capital to secure their market positions.

In response to this intensifying competition, Y Combinator-backed Circleback is introducing a free subscription tier. This new plan allows users to transcribe an unlimited number of meetings, with the caveat that they can only access their meeting history for the last 30 days—a growth-loop tactic previously adopted by rival Granola.

Under the free tier, users can record meetings, utilize mobile and Apple Watch apps, leverage AI to query transcripts, and integrate with Linear and Slack. For advanced users requiring unlimited history, full API, and MCP (Model Context Protocol) access, plans now start at $15 per month (billed annually), down from the previous entry-level price of $20.83 per month with no free tier.

Founded in 2023 by Ali Haghani and Kevin Jacyna, Circleback raised $2.5 million in 2024. The company has been highly profitable since, boasting an exceptional per-employee ARR of over $1 million with a lean team of just eight people, translating to a total ARR of approximately $8 million.

Haghani explained that the previous short-term trial led to high drop-off rates, prompting the pivot to a free tier which acts as their primary marketing engine instead of traditional paid advertising like Google or Meta Ads. With strong unit economics and competitive wins against heavily funded incumbents, the startup is in no rush to raise additional capital.

[AgentUpdate Depth Analysis] #Circleback's pricing restructuring highlights a significant shift in how AI-powered productivity tools capture value. Notably, gating MCP (Model Context Protocol) access behind their paid tier is a highly strategic move. Developed by Anthropic, #MCP acts as an open standard for AI agents to securely query external data sources. By positioning itself as an MCP-compliant data source, Circleback elevates its status from a passive transcription utility to an active context provider for broader AI Agent workflows. This forward-looking approach enables downstream AI agents to seamlessly consume structured meeting intelligence, illustrating how modern SaaS applications must evolve from isolated UI platforms into interoperable knowledge nodes within the emerging Agentic ecosystem.
