SpaceX, Anthropic, and OpenAI Gear Up for a Hot IPO Summer

June 13, 2026|3 Min Read
#openai#anthropic#ipo#ai infrastructure

The IPO market is staging a major comeback, but a new guard is leading the charge. The era of FAANG has run its course, and a new power acronym is taking over: MANGOS — representing Meta (or Microsoft), Anthropic, Nvidia, Google, OpenAI, and SpaceX. With half of this elite group marching toward the public markets in the same tight window, it serves as a massive stress test for institutional investors, private market valuations, and our fundamental expectations of what a public tech company should look like by 2026.

On this episode of TechCrunch’s Equity podcast, hosts Kirsten Korosec, Anthony Ha, and Sean O’Kane dissect the realities behind the flashiest headline numbers. The discussion digs into who actually stands to benefit from this transitional moment. As pioneer companies scaling generative AI and commercial space infrastructure transition to public scrutiny, the shift from venture-backed growth to quarterly public earnings will redefine tech valuations globally.

[AgentUpdate Depth Analysis] The emergence of the MANGOS cohort signals a massive capital consolidation around the core pillars of the upcoming AI Agent ecosystem. While #OpenAI and #Anthropic represent the cognitive engines of future agentic workflows, SpaceX provides the critical physical-layer telemetry via global satellite networks. Compared to previous SaaS #IPO waves, this movement stress-tests how public markets value massive R&D-heavy cognitive infrastructures that do not yet follow standard GAAP profitability curves. Anthropic’s push for open integrations like Model Context Protocol (MCP) contrasts with OpenAI’s hyper-integrated ecosystem. This public-market push will establish the baseline valuation metrics for autonomous agent technologies, forcing a pivot from superficial app-level investments to robust, standardized agent protocols that can sustain enterprise-grade mission-critical workloads.
