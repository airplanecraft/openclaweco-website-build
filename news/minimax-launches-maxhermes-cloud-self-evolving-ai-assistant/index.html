MiniMax Launches MaxHermes: The World's First Cloud-Based Self-Evolving AI Assistant Built on Hermes Agent

April 16, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#hermes agent#maxhermes#ai assistant#self-evolving ai#cloud sandbox

MiniMax recently announced the official launch of #MaxHermes, the world's first cloud-based sandbox built on its #Hermes Agent. MaxHermes is positioned as a #self-evolving AI assistant designed to enhance the efficiency and intelligence of AI in handling complex tasks.

A key feature of this system is its unique learning loop mechanism. Upon successfully completing a complex task, MaxHermes automatically identifies and extracts reusable "Skills," saving them as independent documents. For subsequent tasks, these saved Skills are loaded on demand. Crucially, MaxHermes continuously refines and optimizes these skills based on new user feedback, enabling constant iteration and enhancement of its capabilities.
