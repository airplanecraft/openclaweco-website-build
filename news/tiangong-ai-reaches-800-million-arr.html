Tiangong AI Hits $800M ARR, Nearing China's First Non-BAT $1B AI Company

July 2, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#generative-ai#ai-agents#arr#multimodal-ai

Kunlun Wanwei announced that its Tiangong AI business achieved a historic breakthrough in Q2 2026, with the Annual Recurring Revenue (#ARR) of its AI Native model and product business surpassing $800 million. Specifically, its blockbuster AI drama platform business contributed over $700 million in ARR, while AI tools topped $100 million. Meanwhile, its drama platform DramaWave has completed an AI-native transition, with over 80% of its newly published content now generated by AI, signaling that the global short drama industry has officially entered the AI-native content era.

While the global AI industry has attracted over $100 billion in funding over the past two years, scaled monetization remains the biggest challenge. Most AI enterprises globally and in China are still stuck in model training, user acquisition, or early-stage validation, heavily relying on venture capital. Companies generating hundreds of millions in revenue are rare, and AI Native startups approaching the $1 billion ARR milestone are practically non-existent.

In this landscape, Tiangong AI has successfully closed the loop from model capabilities and product matrices to substantial revenue. Its video generation model SkyReels, music model Mureka, and world model MatrixGame have consecutively topped international benchmarks. The company has built a robust ecosystem spanning content creation, entertainment, and gaming, maintaining strong double-digit QoQ growth and leading globally across model capability, product adoption, and monetization.

Since founder Yahui Zhou took over as CEO of Tiangong AI in Q4 2025, the company has heavily accelerated commercialization. Zhou remarked: "AI is transitioning from a technological revolution to an industrial revolution—moving from labs to real users, and from models to revenues." Tiangong AI is now on track to secure China's first-ever "non-BAT $1 billion ARR" milestone in the AI sector.

[AgentUpdate Depth Analysis] Tiangong AI's explosive ARR growth is not just a victory for multimodal AI, but also provides a brilliant blueprint for the AI Agent ecosystem. While traditional Agent frameworks often position AI as back-office co-pilots with limited monetization caps, Tiangong AI has weaponized multimodal generation (video, audio, text) into fully autonomous "Content Generation Agents" embedded directly within high-frequency consumption platforms like DramaWave. Cross-comparing with western counterparts like Character.ai or Midjourney, Tiangong AI bypasses the "tool trap" by constructing an integrated "agent-to-consumer" pipeline. This demonstrates how AI Agents are evolving from single-purpose assistants into fully autonomous distribution platforms. This vertical integration of Agent generation and algorithmic distribution is highly disruptive, signaling a major acceleration towards a mature Agent Economy.
