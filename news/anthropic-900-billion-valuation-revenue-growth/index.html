Anthropic Targets $900 Billion Valuation Driven by $14 Billion Annual Revenue

May 13, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#anthropic#claude#claude code#generative ai#enterprise ai

Anthropic is demonstrating phenomenal momentum by reportedly seeking a $900 billion valuation in early discussions for a new funding round. The company is looking to raise at least $30 billion, a staggering leap that comes just months after its record-breaking Series G financing. This valuation reflects the market's immense confidence in #Anthropic's trajectory within the #generative AI landscape.

The core driver behind this valuation is Anthropic's explosive financial growth. The company’s annualized revenue has recently surged to $14 billion, showcasing massive enterprise adoption. Analysis of the revenue stream reveals that a significant 80% originates from enterprise customers, highlighting the stability and scalability of their B2B operations.

Technologically, the company's AI coding tool, #Claude Code, has emerged as a massive financial engine. It is currently generating $2.5 billion in annualized revenue on its own. By successfully monetizing high-intent developer tools and enterprise-grade models, Anthropic is rapidly solidifying its position as a dominant force in the industry.
