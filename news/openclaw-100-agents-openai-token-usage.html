OpenClaw Team Manages 100 Coding Agents with $1.3M Monthly Token Usage

May 17, 2026|3 Min Read
#openclaw#ai agents#codex#autonomous coding#openai

The #OpenClaw team has pushed the boundaries of AI-driven software development by demonstrating the immense scalability of #autonomous coding systems. A lean team of just three people successfully managed an armada of 100 autonomous coding agents, showcasing a revolutionary shift in software development productivity.

Operational data reveals that the project consumed a staggering 603 billion tokens through 7.6 million requests in just 30 days. This massive workload was handled by approximately 100 Codex-driven instances working on the OpenClaw Open Source project. The resulting API bill, totaling approximately $1.3 million, is being fully sponsored by #OpenAI to fuel cutting-edge innovation in the developer ecosystem.

This milestone offers a glimpse into a future where Open Source projects can achieve superhuman efficiency. By leveraging generative AI at this scale, small teams can now execute complex development tasks that previously required massive engineering departments. The OpenClaw experiment underscores the viability of high-density agent orchestration and its potential to redefine real-world software engineering workflows.
