Unitree Launches $540K Manned Mecha; Kuaishou's Kling AI Eyes Funding

May 13, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#unitree#kling-ai#samsung-electronics#mecha-robotics#generative-ai

Unitree Robotics has officially unveiled the GD01, a manned transformable mecha with a starting price of 3.9 million RMB (approx. $540,000). Positioned as the world's first mass-produced manned mecha, the GD01 weighs approximately 500kg and features real-time pilot operation and transformation capabilities. Investors emphasize that this product serves as a showcase for #Unitree's technological prowess in sensors, motor controllers, and complex mechanical engineering, utilizing a high-profile use case to market its core robotics stack.

Kuaishou is reportedly exploring strategic options for its video generation model, Kling AI. The company confirmed it is evaluating a restructuring plan that may involve seeking external financing and a potential independent listing. This move aligns with the broader industry trend of spinning off high-growth AI assets to accelerate development and capture market valuation in the competitive generative AI landscape.

Samsung Electronics is bracing for a massive labor strike as negotiations between management and the union collapsed. Over 50,000 employees are expected to participate in a total walkout starting May 21 for 18 days. Analysts warn that this could severely disrupt the production of AI-related memory and logic chips. The union's core demand includes a revised bonus structure linked directly to operating profits rather than the current capped system.

In the social media sector, Tencent's PR head Zhang Jun clarified that WeChat will never implement 'Seen' receipts or 'Visitor History' features, stating these possibilities are permanently 'welded shut' to protect user privacy. Meanwhile, China's market regulator has conditionally approved Tencent’s acquisition of a stake in Ximalaya, imposing five major restrictions to prevent monopolistic behavior, such as banning exclusive copyright deals and bundling services with automotive platforms.

The Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) has mandated that short video platforms implement six mandatory labels. Most notably, creators must now tag 'AI-generated content' to ensure transparency. Other regulatory updates include the standardization of labels for fictionalized accounts and commercial promotions. On the economic front, domestic airlines are set to raise fuel surcharges starting May 16, even as base airfares hit seasonal lows with discounts reaching 80-90% for popular routes post-holiday season.
