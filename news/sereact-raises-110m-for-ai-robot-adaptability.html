German AI Robotics Startup Sereact Raises $110M Series B to Boost Robot Adaptability

April 28, 2026 | 3 Min Read
#robotics#ai agents#funding#intelligent robots#sereact

Sereact, a German #robotics software company, has announced the successful completion of a $110 million Series B #funding round. The capital infusion is earmarked for the development of its artificial intelligence models, designed to significantly enhance robot intelligence and adaptability across various tasks.

The funding round was led by prominent venture capital firm Headline, with participation from new investors including Bullhound Capital, Felix Capital, and Daphni. Existing backers also continued their support.

Sereact's primary objective is to overcome the current limitations of robotic systems in unstructured and dynamic environments. By leveraging its advanced AI models, robots will be empowered to better comprehend complex situations, learn autonomously, and adapt to evolving operational demands, unlocking greater utility in sectors such as manufacturing, logistics, and warehousing.
